The short version

Download the mods from this website and enjoy them in your own game. To show a friend, send them a link to this site rather than the file. The mods are closed source and can never be sold or used to make money in any way.

License

Every mod on this site is All Rights Reserved (ARR). It's the standard license Minecraft modders use for closed-source mods, and it means I keep every right to my mods. Nobody can copy, share, sell or change them unless I give written permission.

The one permission I do give is below: you can download the mods here and use them in your own game. If anything on this page and the license text ever disagree, the license wins.

Read the license text

What you can do

  • Download any mod from this website for free.
  • Use it in your own Minecraft game, for your own private enjoyment.
  • Show it in screenshots, videos and streams, as long as you don't share the file and you point people to this website to download it.

What you can't do

Unless I've given you written permission, you can't:

  • Share the files. Don't reupload, mirror or send the mod files to anyone, whether publicly or privately. That includes other websites, launchers, mod platforms, Discord servers, and modpacks you share with others. Share a link to this website instead.
  • Sell or make money from them. The mods can't be sold, rented, put behind a paywall, ad-link or link shortener, offered as a donation or subscription reward, bundled with anything that's sold, or used in any other paid way.
  • Pull them apart. Don't decompile, reverse engineer or edit the mods, and don't copy their code, textures or other assets into your own projects.
  • Claim them as your own. Don't remove my name or the license from a mod, or present any part of one as your own work.

If you break these rules, your permission to use the mods ends and you'll need to delete them.

Client-side use

Every mod here is client-side: it runs only in your own game and is never installed on a server. You can use the mods while playing on servers, but it's up to you to follow each server's rules about which mods are allowed.

No warranty

The mods are provided as they are, without any warranty. I test them carefully, but I can't promise they'll work with every other mod or never cause a problem, and I'm not responsible for crashes, lost data or other damage. Always back up your worlds before adding or updating mods.

Privacy

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or any kind of tracking, and it doesn't collect information about you. The download numbers you see are simply the totals GitHub keeps for each file. Your browser asks GitHub for those numbers when a page loads, the same way it loads any web page.

Code of conduct

These rules apply in the Discord and anywhere else you're talking with me or other players about the mods.

Please

  • Be respectful and patient. Everyone was new to mods once.
  • Keep bug reports and suggestions clear, constructive and on topic.
  • Help other players when you can.

Don't

  • Harass, insult or threaten anyone, or use hateful language about anyone's identity.
  • Spam, advertise, or post links to pirated software, cheats, malware or reuploaded mod files.
  • Share NSFW content or anyone's private information.
  • Demand updates or new features. The mods are made in my spare time.

Breaking these rules can get your messages removed or you muted or banned. Discord's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines also apply on the server.

Changes

I may update these terms and the license from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when they last changed. Questions? Ask on Discord.
