Using the mods

Every mod on this site is free. You can download them, play with them in singleplayer, and run them on your own or your friends' servers, including public servers.

Modpacks & videos

  • You can include any of my mods in a modpack, public or private, on any platform.
  • You can feature the mods in videos, streams and screenshots, monetised or not.
  • A credit or a link back to this site is appreciated but not required.

What's not allowed

  • Reuploading my mod files to other sites as standalone downloads. Link to this page or the official download instead, so players always get the latest, safe version.
  • Claiming the mods, or any part of them, as your own work.
  • Selling the mods, or putting them behind a paywall or ad-link.
  • Bundling the mods with malware, cheats or anything that harms players.

Source code

Where a mod's source code is public, its license (listed on the mod's page) decides what you can do with the code. If a mod has no public source, please ask before reusing any of it.

No warranty

The mods are provided as they are, without any warranty. I test them carefully, but I can't guarantee they'll work with every other mod or never cause problems. Always back up your worlds before adding, updating or removing mods.

Code of conduct

This applies in the Discord, on GitHub, and anywhere else you're talking with me or other players about these mods.

Do

  • Be respectful and patient. Everyone was new to modding once.
  • Keep bug reports and suggestions constructive and on topic.
  • Help other players when you can.

Don't

  • Harass, insult or threaten anyone, or use hateful language about anyone's identity.
  • Spam, advertise, or post links to pirated software, cheats or malware.
  • Share NSFW content or other people's private information.
  • Demand updates or ports. They're made in my spare time.

Breaking these rules can get your messages removed, or you muted or banned from the community. Discord's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines also apply on the server.

Changes

I may update these terms from time to time. The date at the top shows when they last changed. Questions? Ask on Discord.
